# Brand — Everspan

Everspan separates yield-bearing assets into tradable principal and yield positions on Stellar.

_Originally generated by `brand-design` on 2026-08-01 (palette "Ember"). Rebuilt as black and
white on 2026-09-10; this document describes that system._

## Palette — Black and white, two action hues

**Vibe:** calm · exact · banking-app · serious
**Category:** DeFi
**Mood:** ink on paper, with colour only where the reader commits or undoes something

The interface is a greyscale ramp with a measured chroma of exactly zero. Exactly two hues are
spent, and only on meaning a reader acts on:

- **Green** — an action that commits or acquires, and the success that follows it.
- **Red** — an action that destroys or reverses, and the failure that follows it.

Everything else is black, white or a grey between them: a primary button is simply the ink, a
warning is grey, a concept icon is grey. The one deliberate exception is **token marks** (below),
which are asset identity rather than interface colour.

`src/lib/theme.test.ts` enforces the split — every theme token is either achromatic or one of the
two action families — and pins the contrast ratios in the table further down.

### Surfaces

| Surface                         | Selector                          | Ground            | Ink       |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------- | --------- |
| App, dark (default)             | `.surface-app[data-theme='dark']` | `#000000`, panels `#1B1B1B` | `#FFFFFF` |
| App, light                      | `.surface-app[data-theme='light']`| `#F2F2F2`, cards `#FFFFFF`  | `#000000` |
| Marketing route                 | `.surface-site`                   | `#F2F2F2` paper   | `#000000` |
| Dark chapter inside marketing   | `.surface-ink`                    | black             | white     |

On the dark app the ramp steps are spaced by perceptual lightness rather than equal channel
values — near black, equal steps are invisible and a card sinks into the page. On the light app
the page keeps a 4% grey so an elevated card can be pure white and separate by being lighter.

### Theme tokens (`src/index.css`)

Tokens are `R G B` triples consumed as `rgb(var(--token) / <alpha>)` through `tailwind.config.js`.

| Token family        | Job                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `neutral-50…950`    | The greyscale ramp. `50` is the loudest ink, `950` the page.        |
| `boundary`          | Edge of an interactive control; carries 3:1 non-text contrast.      |
| `hairline`          | Decorative edge — card outlines and dividers.                       |
| `raised`            | Fill of a selected, hovered or skeleton control.                    |
| `accent-100…600`    | The ink itself: white on the dark app, black on paper. `on-accent` is its opposite. |
| `positive-100…500`  | Green. `-300` carries text on a card; `-500` is a button fill holding white type. |
| `negative-100…500`  | Red, built the same way.                                            |
| `warning-100…500`   | Grey. A caution attached to an action is not an action, so it earns no hue — it separates by weight, icon and wording. |
| `figure-*`          | Four legacy names (`ember`, `ochre`, `verdigris`, `mulberry`) all set to one grey. Kept so the call sites stay put and a return to concept colour is one CSS block, not forty components. |
| `brand-*`           | `--brand-paper #FFFFFF`, `--brand-ink #000000` and friends, for the few places that need a literal (selection, `html` background). |

### Token marks — the one place colour is decoration

Balances and positions are drawn with a coin: a solid disc in the asset's colour with a flat
glyph on top, the way an exchange app shows coin logos on a neutral interface. They are identity,
not interface, so their colours live in `src/lib/tokenMarks.ts` — never in `src/index.css`.

| Mark        | Fill      | Glyph     | Used for                                   |
| ----------- | --------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `xlm`       | `#1E2127` | `#FFFFFF` | XLM (Stellar mark), the Blend market underlying |
| `musdy`     | `#2F6BEF` | `#FFFFFF` | mUSDY, the default market underlying        |
| `principal` | `#7A48E0` | `#FFFFFF` | Principal (PT)                             |
| `yield`     | `#F2A93B` | `#2A1700` | Yield (YT) and claimable yield             |
| `liquidity` | `#0E8FBF` | `#FFFFFF` | LP shares                                  |

Rules, enforced by `src/lib/tokenMarks.test.ts`:

- Every coloured fill sits at least **30° of OKLab hue** away from both green and red, so a coin
  can never be mistaken for a confirm or a failure.
- Every glyph holds **3:1** against its own fill.
- A hairline ring in `neutral-50/15` separates the graphite coin from a black page and the amber
  coin from a white one.

**Where they go:** beside an amount of that token — balance rows, position figures, review lines,
the activity feed, the market switcher. **Where they don't:** action shortcuts and strategy tiles
(Lock, Increase yield, Add liquidity) are things you do, not things you hold; they keep grey
concept icons. Components use `TokenIcon` / `TokenAmount` from `src/components/TokenIcon.tsx`.

## Naming

Primary UI says **Principal** and **Yield** (Turkish: Anapara, Kazanç), not PT/YT; SY is never
named in the primary flow — amounts denominated in SY are shown as their underlying equivalent.
PT/YT appear only in advanced surfaces (Split/Merge, Pool). Translations are matched on exact
English strings in `src/i18n/translations.ts`: change a string, re-key its entry.

## Typography

- **Display + body:** Space Grotesk Variable (`font-sans`).
- **Balance figures:** the platform UI face (`font-figure` → SF Pro / Segoe UI / Roboto), bold,
  `-0.02em` tracking, tabular digits. The fraction and unit ride the whole number's baseline as
  one smaller, quieter run (`2,366` `.58 mUSDY`). Used for the Home total value.
- **Mono:** system monospace stack, for amounts in dense rows and review lines (`tabular-nums`).
- Headlines are large, compact and restrained.

## Controls

- **Material:** every button variant except `ghost` is a squircle (`rounded-[18px]`) of
  translucent blurred fill with a hairline top highlight. `ghost` — header icons, a sheet's
  dismiss — sits on bare canvas with no fill.
- **Variants carry meaning:** `primary` is colourless (the ink); `positive` is green, for the
  commit; `danger` is red, for the reversal; `secondary` and `warning` are grey. A "Continue" never
  borrows green.
- **Motion:** no press treatment; a `hover:scale-[1.04]` zoom on devices that hover only.
- **Focus:** `outline` with offset (`focusRing`), not `ring`, so the real backdrop shows through.
- **Radii:** buttons `18px`, segmented options `14px`, rows/inputs `rounded-xl`, panels
  `rounded-2xl`, ghost icons `rounded-full`.
- **Hit area:** 44px minimum; the 36px `sm` size extends its target with a pseudo-element.

## Tone and voice

Use direct, factual language. Describe the position, rate and maturity before describing the
technology. Prefer short sentences and concrete numbers.

Avoid hype, urgency, exclamation marks and claims such as "revolutionary", "effortless" or
"risk-free". Never imply guaranteed protocol safety or guaranteed yield.

> One deposit. Two positions. Choose the rate exposure you want to hold.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Keep one primary (ink) action per viewport whenever possible.
- Spend green on the commit and red on the reversal or failure — and nowhere else in the interface.
- Put a token coin beside an amount of that token; keep concept icons grey.
- Use a 4 px spacing grid and large, intentional whitespace.
- Carry meaning with icons and explicit copy as well as hue, never by hue alone.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep all content usable without animation.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode interface colours in components; use the Tailwind tokens. Token-mark colours are the
  single exception, and they live in `src/lib/tokenMarks.ts`.
- Use green or red for market *direction*. A rate below zero is marked with a sign glyph, not a colour.
- Add a third interface hue — for warnings, highlights or concept icons.
- Give a token coin a hue within 30° of green or red.
- Paint decorative gradients or heavy shadows. The glass buttons are the one material effect.
- Copy Paribu assets, text or exact layouts; translate the visual principles into Everspan's product story.
